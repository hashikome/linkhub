# ADR-0058: Curated guides as the product's defining value

Status: Accepted; working-name decision superseded by [ADR-0059](0059-ntumie-product-name.md)
Date: 2026-10-12

## Context

The existing Guides North Star makes collections into maintained guides. While
considering Goodlinks as a name derived from the slogan, the author discovered
the existing GoodLinks application. Comparing its article-reading emphasis with
LinkHub's direction made the collection's purpose easier to express.

## Decision

Treat the maintained collection and the context between its resources as the
product's defining value. Saving a link is an entry action. Owners supply
selection, order and explanation; readers discover or receive a guide, explore
its destinations and can return to the same maintained address.

Keep “Good links belong together.” as the slogan and LinkHub as the working name.
A distinct final name remains open. Document the comparison fairly: GoodLinks
also organizes and shares links; its creator's personal motivations and naming
story are not established by its marketing material.

Use [product-positioning.md](../design-docs/product-positioning.md) as the focused
reference for feature, interface and copy decisions. PRODUCT.md remains the
canonical authority for requirements and delivery status. This elaborates the
existing north star; it supersedes no access, pricing or architectural decision.

## Consequences

Evaluate work through the usefulness of the collection to its owner and reader.
Preserve readable authored context, comfortable return from resources, durable
sharing and explicit access rules. Do not infer demand, publication readiness or
implemented capabilities from a positioning statement. A future rename is a
separate decision and implementation task.
